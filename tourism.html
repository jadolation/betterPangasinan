About this guide and its sources

Descriptions come from the provincial tourism office (See Pangasinan) and the provincial government. Commute steps come from official LGU pages where available, otherwise from Wikivoyage. When we could not find a source for a step, the route says so instead of guessing. Fares and schedules change; the guide does not publish a fare without an as-of date.

Photos are copied from Wikimedia Commons under their stated licenses and served from this site. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap; see the map privacy notice. Methodology · Download the dataset · Send a correction
