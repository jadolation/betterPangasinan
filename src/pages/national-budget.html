About the Data

The explorer presents a dated, independently compiled comparison of national budget proposals. It is not an official budget publication or a record of spending.

What is the NEP?

The National Expenditure Program is the executive branch's proposed national budget. It is a proposal, not enacted appropriations, an expenditure report or a construction contract.

What is the House bill?

The House proposal is another budget version. DPWH comparisons use the third reading; farm-to-market roads, health facilities and irrigation use the second reading. These readings remain separate. Never add NEP and House amounts together.

What do House bill only and NEP only mean?

These are upstream comparison candidates: a matching line was not found in the other version. They do not independently prove a legislative insertion or deletion, concealment or wrongdoing. Unchanged, increased and decreased categories also retain upstream pairing warnings.

Why do changes differ from the difference between totals?

Each version's sum includes its known amounts only. Missing values remain unknown, not zero. Comparable-row change uses records with both amounts and excludes held and different-scope comparisons. Every aggregate reports known-row counts, and House readings are never combined into one financial total.

What is a shared allocation?

Some lines fund several areas or belong to broader office scopes. Their entire amount cannot be assigned to Pangasinan or one municipality. Office, shared, uncertain and reviewed geographic lenses are different, potentially overlapping views, not amounts to add together.

What does geographic verification establish?

Only separately reviewed evidence can support an LGU association. Name searches may include places outside Pangasinan, such as New Pangasinan in South Cotabato. An office jurisdiction is not a verified project location. A reviewed status does not establish exhaustive coverage of a province or LGU.

How were the records collected and approved?

A Python pipeline paginated province-name and exact engineering-office searches in the BetterGov.ph Budget API, preserved source responses and project details, and deduplicated dataset/project identifiers. Publication decisions bind the snapshot and record hashes. Publication approval, source warnings and geographic review remain independent; this interface does not change those decisions.

How should offices and districts be interpreted?

Some offices serve several districts. Shared-office budgets remain undivided and must not be counted once for each district. Whole-office NEP and named-project comparisons are different scopes. Historical or undated amounts retained as office-mapping evidence are not FY2027 project allocations or a published historical-budget dataset.

What does this dataset not prove?

It does not establish actual spending, an enacted appropriation, a contractor, construction progress, completion, corruption or which elected official controlled a project. No match does not prove no funding. Historical GAA records and local-government finances are separate datasets; they are not added to these proposals.

Which sources were used, and when was the snapshot collected?

See the publication manifest for the source revision and retrieval date. No live API response is assumed.

Source links lead to the original NEP and House documents wherever provided. The BetterGov.ph API documentation explains the upstream service. SHA-256 checks establish consistency with the publication manifest, not independent source authenticity.
