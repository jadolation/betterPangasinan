Corrections

Evidence comes first

A portal summary can be incomplete or wrong. The cited source and reporting context must remain visible.

What needs review

Errors may involve a transcription, broken source link, wrong reporting period, unit, relationship, or interpretation. A source's later revision may also require an update. Missing portal data is not evidence of wrongdoing or failure to disclose.

How corrections should be made

Maintainers should compare the published record with its cited document, record the reason and supporting evidence in the source change history, and rerun validation before republishing. Unsupported or misleading records should be withheld while reviewed rather than replaced with guessed values. Material changes should identify what changed and why alongside the affected record.

No submission channel yet

A dedicated public corrections channel has not been published. This site does not accept reports, documents, or personal submissions, and makes no response-time promise. Contributors with repository access should use its review process and public source references, not private documents or unsupported allegations.

For official determinations, consult the responsible agency and original documents. Read our methodology and privacy policy.
