About the Project

One province. Accessible information.

Better Pangasinan is an independent, volunteer-led civic technology initiative helping people explore public information across Pangasinan.

Our vision is accessible information and accountable governance. This is not an official government website. We do not claim government endorsement or a formal affiliation with any government agency or civic organization.

Our Focus

A provincial view, with local context

Explore LGUs, finances, projects, procurement, and public resources in one place. Records need sources, dates, and clear responsibility: a project's location alone does not establish which office funds or implements it.

Evidence before numbers

Where reviewed records are not yet available, we say so rather than inventing figures or presenting missing values as zero. Missing portal records do not establish that an LGU has failed to disclose information. Read our methodology for sourcing and availability rules.
