Methodology & Sources

Public information, with context

A source, a reporting period, and clear limits matter more than a convenient number.

What we publish

Public records must have traceable source references and pass review before publication. Source registration, normalization, validation, and human approval precede the public build. Automated checks cannot establish that a source is accurate or complete.

Where records are available, consult their cited documents, responsible entities, reporting periods, and review status. Publication here is not government certification. Synthetic test records are not public evidence and are excluded from production.

Reading availability

Not collected means the portal has not yet gathered a reviewed record. Source unavailable means a needed source could not be accessed. Neither means zero, no activity, or LGU noncompliance. Place names used for navigation do not verify population, classification, boundaries, or identifiers.

Comparing money and projects

Appropriations, actual expenditure, approved budgets, and contract values describe different things. Compare only compatible reporting years, units, and amount types. Unknown amounts remain unknown, not zero. Project geography alone does not identify its funding or implementing office.

National budget proposals

The national budget explorer uses separately reviewed snapshots of the independent, unofficial BetterGov.ph Budget API. FY2027 NEP and House figures are two versions of a proposed budget, never money to add together and never proof of spending, a contract award, or completed work. DPWH uses the House third reading; farm-to-market roads, health facilities and irrigation use the second reading. Amounts missing from a version remain unknown.

Name-search matches are collection leads, not verified physical locations. Central and regional office lines may cover several provinces. Engineering-office totals remain separate from reviewed geographic matches; shared offices are counted once, never once per district served. Municipal views may overlap. Whole-office NEP figures are not directly comparable to House named-project figures. Coverage and comparable-row changes are labeled separately.

Source comparison warnings and geographic review are independent of publication approval. Candidate pairings, insertions and deletions do not establish wrongdoing. Every public snapshot must retain the upstream notices, original source-document links and its retrieval date. A failed refresh does not replace an approved snapshot; no approved data means unavailable, not zero funding. Historical enacted GAA records are deferred and are not mixed with proposals.

Limits and updates

Coverage is incomplete. Reviewed official identifiers, boundaries, financial records, and translations may be unavailable. English is the published interface; additional language foundations are not a claim of reviewed translations. Source documents remain the reference for legal or administrative decisions.

See our corrections policy for how errors should be assessed and our privacy policy before following external links.
